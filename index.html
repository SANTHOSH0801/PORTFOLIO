05 / ComponentsMaster Filter V2+

A reusable, config-driven filter layer — sync, async and table filters — built on the internal XUI library. New screens add filters through config instead of custom code, cutting feature development time.

06 / InfrastructureFour packages, one monorepo+

Restructured the monorepo into a single package with one audited dependency manifest for two production apps (DTX and GTX), removing redundant packages and simplifying builds and deployments.

07 / Admin toolingUser management+

Add, edit, delete and lock/unlock users across every role — vendors, financiers, Risk and Sales admins — with role-based access and a sortable, filterable list view.

08 / SchedulingHoliday Calendar for financiers+

Custom holidays and configurable weekends, with backend-calculated, holiday-adjusted disbursement dates surfaced during bidding to prevent payment-scheduling errors.

09 / GuardrailsRisk & compliance checks+

RBI-compliant parent–child entity hierarchies for buyers and financiers, buyer/seller active-limit checks before any bid, re-bid or approval, and unified credit-rating capture.
